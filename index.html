To My Mauu,

There are billions of people in this world.

Billions of hearts.
Billions of dreams.
Billions of stories waiting to be written.

Yet somehow, in this unimaginably huge universe, my story found yours.

And I think that's the most beautiful miracle that has ever happened to me.

Sometimes I wonder what my life would have looked like if I had never met you.

Would I still laugh the way I do now?

Would ordinary days still feel this beautiful?

Would I know what it feels like to have someone who makes the entire world seem a little softer just by existing?

Honestly...

I don't think so.

Because before you became my girlfriend...

Before I ever called you "my love"...

Before I even realized what my heart was doing...

You became my best friend.

And I don't think you'll ever understand how much that means to me.

You became the person I wanted to tell everything to.

The first person I wanted to text whenever something happened.

The first person whose opinion mattered to me.

The first person who could make me smile without even trying.

I never planned to fall in love with you.

It just...

Happened.

So quietly that I didn't even notice.

One conversation became another.

One laugh became a memory.

One memory became a hundred.

Until one day I realized...

My happiest moments always had you in them.

People talk about love at first sight.

But I think what we have is even more beautiful.

Because I didn't fall in love with a face.

I fell in love with your heart.

With your kindness.

With the way you care.

With the way you listen.

With the way you make me feel safe enough to be completely myself.

I fell in love with the person you are.

And every single day, I discover another reason to love you even more.

Mauu...

Do you know what you've become to me?

You've become the first thought that crosses my mind every morning.

The smile that appears for no reason.

The voice that calms every storm inside me.

The name I search for before anyone else's.

The person I miss even five minutes after we've stopped talking.

Sometimes I catch myself smiling at my phone before I've even opened your message.

Sometimes I reread our old chats just to relive the happiness they gave me.

Sometimes I'll hear a song, see a sunset, or notice something funny...

And my very first thought is,

"I can't wait to tell Mauu."

You've become part of every little piece of my life.

Not because I forced you into it.

But because my heart quietly made space for you everywhere.

Thank you...

For every late-night conversation that lasted until we forgot what time it was.

For every "good morning" that made my day start with a smile.

For every "good night" that somehow made sleeping easier.

For every laugh.

Every memory.

Every silly argument.

Every apology.

Every moment we chose to understand each other instead of giving up.

Thank you for making love feel gentle.

Before you, I thought love had to be loud.

I thought it had to be dramatic.

You taught me something completely different.

Love is asking if the other person has eaten.

Love is remembering little details.

Love is waiting safely until the other person gets home.

Love is staying on a call after saying goodbye five different times.

Love is laughing over jokes nobody else understands.

Love is looking at one person and thinking,

"Life became better because you exist."

That's what you've been for me.

Better.

You didn't change who I was.

You simply made me love the person I became when I was with you.

I know we've only been together for two months.

To the world...

That's just sixty days.

But to me...

It's sixty days of feeling happier than I ever imagined.

Sixty days of learning that home isn't always a place.

Sometimes...

Home is a person.

And for me...

Home has your smile.

Home has your voice.

Home has your heart.

Home has your name.

If someone gave me the chance to restart my life from the very beginning...

I wouldn't ask for more money.

Or more success.

Or an easier life.

I'd ask for one thing.

To find you again.

I'd still become your best friend.

I'd still stay awake talking to you.

I'd still laugh at your jokes.

I'd still annoy you.

I'd still protect you.

I'd still fall in love with you.

Again.

And again.

And again.

Because in every lifetime...

I'd still choose you.

Not because I have to.

Because there has never been anyone else my heart has wanted more.

I know we're still young.

I know we don't know what every chapter of our future will look like.

Life will surprise us.

Sometimes it'll be beautiful.

Sometimes it'll be difficult.

But I hope one thing never changes.

I hope we never stop being best friends.

Because that's where our love was born.

And I think that's the strongest kind of forever anyone could ever ask for.

Before I finish this letter...

There's something I want to say.

Not to "my girlfriend."

Not even to "my best friend."

But to Aarya.

Thank you.

Thank you for existing.

Thank you for walking into my life without realizing you would become my greatest happiness.

Thank you for showing me that sometimes the greatest love stories don't begin with "I love you."

Sometimes...

They begin with,

"Hi."

Then become,

"You're my best friend."

And one day...

Without either person noticing...

They become,

"You're my home."

Happy Two Months, my Mauu.

If one day, years from now, someone asks me what the greatest blessing of my life was...

I won't have to think.

I'll simply smile...

Remember every conversation, every laugh, every memory we've shared...

And answer with one name.

Aarya.

Because before you became the love of my life...

You became my favourite person.

And I hope that no matter how many years pass, no matter how much the world changes, and no matter where life takes us...

You'll always be my Mauu.

Forever and always yours,
Rajdeep ❤️
